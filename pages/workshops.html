Good to know

Why beginners start here

  1. Safety comes first, not last

    Hygiene, cross-contamination and skin safety are built into every session rather than bolted on at the end.

  2. Genuinely no experience required

    Premade stencils remove the drawing barrier, so your attention goes entirely into technique and depth control.

  3. Small groups, real feedback

    Sessions stay small enough that Van can watch your hand and correct as you go.
