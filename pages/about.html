About Van

Teaching what she practices.

Van Ngo is the artist and teacher behind Vie Glow Academy Inc. She splits her time between guiding beginners through their first tattoos and taking on a small number of fine-line, minimalist client pieces herself.

Why she teaches

Van built her workshops around the idea that tattooing shouldn't feel gatekept. Using premade stencils and a safety-first curriculum, she gives complete beginners a real, guided starting point — no experience necessary.

Why she still tattoos

Alongside teaching, Van keeps a small, intentional client roster for fine-line and minimalist work — the same craft she teaches, practiced at a slower, more personal pace.

Based in

British Columbia & Winnipeg

Workshops run across BC, with sessions also offered in Winnipeg. Client tattoo bookings are based in Vancouver, BC.
