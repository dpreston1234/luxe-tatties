## Target audience
Two distinct audiences the site needs to speak to without confusing them:
- **Aspiring tattoo artists** — beginners with no experience, considering a career change or side skill, price-sensitive enough to want financing, need reassurance on safety/legitimacy
- **Tattoo clients** — people wanting a fine-line/minimalist tattoo done by Van directly, design-conscious, likely first-timers to tattoos generally

## Brand tone
- **Minimalist** — clean, uncluttered, restrained visual language
- **Intimate** — small-studio, personal, mentor-style relationship (fits both a solo teacher and a solo/micro-studio artist)
- **Refined** — quiet-luxury execution: considered typography, generous white space, quality photography

## Color & font direction
No logo or formal brand guide was located. Recommendation based on category research and the "luxe" positioning (to be confirmed against actual @vieglow.ca / @luxetatties visuals once Dez or the client can share screenshots — Instagram's post grid wasn't fetchable via this research pass):
- **Palette**: warm off-white/near-white background, deep charcoal or near-black for text/linework, one muted accent (dusty rose, sage, or soft terracotta) used sparingly
- **Typography**: refined serif for headings (evokes "luxe"/boutique/academy credibility), clean sans-serif for body and UI
- **Imagery**: large-format photography — healed fine-line tattoo work for the client-facing side, workshop/training-in-progress shots for the course side. Avoid stock tattoo imagery.

## Competitor findings

**Training/workshop side:**
- **Van Tattoo Studio** (Vancouver) — offers Full Course (8-day intensive) and Weekend Course (mentorship-paced) apprenticeship-style programs; covers drawing fundamentals, bloodborne pathogen/hygiene training, portfolio review, ongoing mentorship post-graduation
- **HD Beauty Academy** — 2-day "Mini Tattoo Course" for beginners, design-to-live-practice format
- **Noble Art Academy** (Montreal) — hands-on + theory training, includes a kit with stencil materials and synthetic practice skins
- Pattern: competitors lean on **credibility signals** (curriculum structure, safety/hygiene training, mentorship, kit inclusions) — Luxe Tatties' premade-stencil, low-barrier-to-entry angle is a genuine differentiator worth foregrounding

**Client tattoo side:**
- **Pacific Rose Tattoo** (Gastown) — multi-artist studio, fine line among other styles, strong neighborhood branding
- **Miki S Tattoo** — Vancouver fine-line/single-needle/micro-realism specialist, symbolism-driven design language — closest model for a focused solo-artist portfolio
- **Adrenaline VanCity** — early fine-line mover in Vancouver, but larger/more commercial feel — useful contrast for how a boutique micro-studio should read smaller and more personal
- Pattern across category: minimalist layout, gallery-driven portfolio as centerpiece, large well-lit photography, simple booking CTA, mobile-first

## Compliance flags
- Tattooing is not a licensed profession in BC; studios are subject to health authority inspection (Fraser Health / Island Health / Vancouver Coastal Health) under Guidelines for Personal Service Establishments. No specific website disclosure mandate found, but recommended for the client-tattoo side:
  - Aftercare information section
  - No medical/health claims
  - Clear age policy (18+) if minors aren't served
- **Training-side compliance considerations (new, from the dual-offering scope):**
  - If the workshops teach tattooing to people without a professional license/oversight, consider whether the site should include a liability waiver, disclaimer that graduates are responsible for meeting their own jurisdiction's requirements before tattooing others, and any age minimum for enrollment
  - If financing (Medicard-style) is offered, standard disclosure practices for third-party financing terms should point to the financing provider's own terms rather than being stated as Luxe Tatties/Vie Glow Academy policy
